The "True Value" of Hand Embroidery

Hand embroidery is one of Pakistan's most intricate and time-consuming traditional crafts. Skilled women artisans spend days-or even weeks-carefully stitching each design by hand, using techniques that have been passed down through generations. Every thread is placed with precision, requiring exceptional patience, creativity, and craftsmanship.

Despite the immense effort involved, these beautiful creations are often sold at prices far below their true value. One of the biggest reasons is that customers rarely know how much time, skill, and dedication goes into making each piece.

At HaathKaKaam, we believe every handcrafted product deserves recognition and fair appreciation. That's why we share the story behind every embroidered item, including the techniques used, the time required to create it, and the artisan who made it. By educating buyers about the craftsmanship behind each product, we aim to help artisans receive the respect and fair value they truly deserve.
